Know about Products Safety Comparison FACTS & FAQS ## Know About Products Safety BIS safety norms are designed to ensure that electronic products sold in India meet certain safety and quality standards. This includes protection against electric shock and resistance to heat and flames, among other safety requirements. Before purchasing electronic products, including lights, consumers should look for the BIS logo. This indicates that the product has been certified by the Bureau of Indian Standards and meets the necessary safety and quality standards. By purchasing BIS certified products, consumers can be assured that the products they are buying are safe to use and meet certain quality standards. This can help prevent accidents and protect consumers from harm, while also ensuring that the products function properly and have a longer lifespan.
