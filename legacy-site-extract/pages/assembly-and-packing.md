At Sheetal Electrotech, we pride ourselves on our state-of-the-art assembly and packing processes for our batten, panel, and downlight products. Our fully SYSTEMATIC conveyor system is designed to ensure that every product is assembled efficiently and accurately, with a DAILY capacity of up to 100k units. Our assembly conveyor system is equipped with advanced technology and features that allow us to assemble products with precision and consistency. processes and quality control measures in place, we can ensure that every product meets the highest quality standards. After assembly, our packing system takes over, packaging each product in a way that protects it during transportation and storage. Our packing system is designed to be flexible, allowing us to use different materials and packaging configurations based on the specific requirements of each product. To ensure the quality and longevity of our products, we also utilize a aging machine. This machine can handle a voltage range of 100v to 320v, allowing us to test and validate our products under various voltage conditions. By subjecting our products to rigorous testing, we can ensure that they meet the highest standards of durability and reliability. At Sheetal Electrotech, we are committed to providing our customers with high-quality products that meet their needs and exceed their expectations.
